Its endowment is exceptional — about a fifth of the world's freshwater reserves, forests covering close to a tenth of the global total, potash reserves second only to Russia's, the third-largest uranium and fourth-largest proven oil reserves, and thirty-four listed critical minerals (several of which it already produces at world scale), without, however, having built the capacity to process them. The economy is high-income and service-dominated, with four in five jobs outside the goods sector, but it rests on a resource-export base and a manufacturing belt in the Ontario–Quebec corridor; the productivity record has however trailed comparable economies for two decades and roughly three-quarters of merchandise exports going to one neighbour.
