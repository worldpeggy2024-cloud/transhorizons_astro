There are no labels, no draft sentences and no summaries here, by design — a label that ran ahead of
its quote is how fourteen misreadings reached draft 4. Read the quote, not a description of it.

**Conventions**

- **Quote** is a slice of the fetched document text, verified verbatim against it. `[…]` marks an elision.
- **Scope** appears when the quote is *not* about the Webequie Supply Road. Read it before using the quote.
- **Reassembled** marks a quote rebuilt from separate columns or pages of a table. It reads as continuous
  prose but is not one sentence in the document — check the locator before treating it as one.
- **Also cited as** lists claim ids with an identical source and quote.
- **Source's own words (known-errors file)** repeats the wording given in `ring-of-fire-draft-4-known-errors.md`
  for that claim. It is a second rendering; where the two differ in extent, the quote above it governs.
