# 3. What the Ring of Fire is

Candidate claims for each paragraph, generated 2026-09-17 from `ledger.json` and `ring-of-fire-draft-1-reordered.md`.

Each paragraph gets up to **two lists, which must not be read as one**:

1. **Candidates** — the quote shares a distinctive item with the paragraph: a figure, year, date,
   amount, unit or proper name. Ranked by how many items are shared.
2. **Topic candidates — weaker than a shared figure or name.** The quote shares no figure and no name,
   only 2+ content words once stopwords and words common to a quarter of the ledger are removed.
   This pass reaches material a paraphrase would otherwise hide, and it is much noisier. Ranked by shared terms.

**Both lists are pointers, not verdicts.** Nothing here says a quote supports a paragraph; only that the
two mention something in common. Open the quote. Nothing is truncated — a list too long to sit under its
paragraph moves, complete and still ranked, to "Long lists" at the end of this file.

Full quotes, scopes and locators are also in `../ledger-export.md`.
