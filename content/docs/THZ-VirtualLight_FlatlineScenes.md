The professional doorway into the room always wanted. 
Virtual Light / Lou is the other half of the same intent — the science-fiction extrapolation, the visual and felt social consequence rendered in a body. The articles establish what is real and what can be done; Lou carries it into lived terrain. **The whole structure = analysis + deep social extrapolation of climate and ecological disruption, aimed at what can be done today.**
The articles establish what is foreseeable and what adaptation costs; Lou's world shows what happened.

Not the better branch : **documenting fragments that already exist** (the stranded structures, the winter she loves, the Lufa network, the Mohawk lineage, the named makers, the grandmother's honest farm) and asking what they become if **they win — defended, grown, allowed to cohere**.

**The potential five opening scene photographs (Peggy's, Irkutsk/Ulan-Ude 2012):**

Five photographs taken from and around the Trans-Siberian/Mongolian railway that ARE Lou's window:

*Arrival 1 — Peggy, December 6, 2001:*

Cargo ship Canmar Honour, Le Havre → Montreal, officer's cabin, 120kg of luggage. A few weeks after 9/11 — peak security hysteria. Nobody searched her luggage. Nobody went near her cabin. She disembarked early morning, walked *outside the harbour* to make a phone call while waiting for immigration to board and stamp her papers. She could have had anything. The only moment of official interest: a customs officer raising an eyebrow at her declaration listing a full box of 26 stuffed toys. That was it. White, French, educated, every document intact. Invisible.

*Arrival 2 — Peggy's Congolese best friend:*

Immigrated to France as a child with her mother and ~8 brothers and sisters. Lived in France for years. Could never become French — because France requires a birth certificate and nothing else, and hers burned in the fire that destroyed the hospital where she was born in Congo. Couldn't visit cousins one hour away across the US border — Congo is on the US entry restriction list. Desperate. Then finally, Canada. Canadian citizenship. Canadian passport. The fire in Congo stopped following her.

*The system made visible:* The hospital fire in Kinshasa, the US blacklist, the cobalt in every phone battery, Lumumba's assassination — these are not separate events. They are the same story. The fire reached all the way to the US border and said *no* for decades. While 26 stuffed toys sailed through customs uninspected.

Lou knows both arrivals. Lou was probably in that ceremony room. This is not a political statement in Virtual Light — it's a character moment. The data made visible.
