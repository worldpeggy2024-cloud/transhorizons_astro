# TransHorizons — site film
## Production document

One continuous navigation, screen capture only, narration synthesized from the author's own voice. Two deliverables from one capture session: a full version for the site, and a 90-second cut for email and outreach.

**Timed runtime:** about 2 minutes 10 seconds. Scroll shots always run longer in practice than on paper, so budget 2:00 to 2:45 for the finished full version.

---

## Voice design

The site uses different voices for different material, and the film depends on that.

- **Notes articles** — Peggy's own voice
- **Portfolio and analysis articles** — mixed voices selected by content
- **Country reports** — two alternating voices

The film's narration is Peggy's voice, generated in Fish Audio. Because the country reports are read by two other voices, the handover at shot 21 is audible without any further arrangement: narration stops, and two unmistakably different voices take over.

**Consequence for capture.** Shot 21's entry point must be chosen so that a switch between the two report voices falls inside its six seconds. If the alternation runs at section length and the shot starts mid-section, only one voice plays and the design stays invisible. Check where the boundaries fall and set the shot's start position accordingly — it is a configuration value, not a structural change.

---

## Before recording

**Browser.** Clean profile. No extensions, no bookmarks bar, no tab strip. Nothing on screen but the site.

**Two things to keep out of frame.**

1. The analyses grid shows three cards and only two analyses are finished. Do not survey the grid. The Analyses heading appears, and the click goes straight into Resource Civilization. The grid is a click target for one second, not content to be read.
2. Thematic Maps carries five *preview coming soon* and two *in preparation* placeholders against one finished map. Crop shot 29 to the Critical Minerals World Map card alone.

**The globe click (shot 25a).** Rotate slowly and land the click cleanly. A globe that overshoots and comes back reads as fumbling rather than as a feature. It is the hinge of Movement 4.

## Narration generation

22 segments per language, 44 files total. **One file per shot — never one file per movement.** Cloned voices drift over long passages: pacing flattens and emphasis stops tracking meaning. Fresh prosody per segment, and a line that lands wrong can be regenerated alone.

Build every pause in the assembly, not in the model. The segments are placed against the shot durations above and silence fills the gaps.

**Rows to watch.**

- **Shot 8** — the longest sentence in the script and the one most likely to flatten. If it does, split at the colon into two files.
- **Shot 15 (French)** — the enumeration needs a beat before *routes arctiques*. If the model rushes it, generate it alone and slow the list with heavier punctuation rather than commas.

## The 90-second cut

From the same capture, no re-shooting:

**1, 2, 3, 11, 12, 13, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 25a, 29, 30, closing card**

Identity and bilingualism, one analysis with its audio and maps, the report with its reading tools, the globe click, the minerals map. Source-tracing (27) comes out — it is the hardest thing to read at speed. The globe click stays: it is legible in two seconds.

---

## Output

1080p, H.264, under 20 MB where possible. Embedded on the About page with a poster frame. Any external copy stays unlisted, so no recommendation algorithm places this beside unrelated content.

Hard cuts throughout. No music — the only sound in the film is narration and, at shot 21, the report's own two voices.
