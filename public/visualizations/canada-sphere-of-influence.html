① Arctic Council

Intergovernmental forum established 1996. Addresses environmental protection and sustainable development in the Arctic. Decisions by consensus among 8 member states.

8 Member States: Canada · United States · Russia · Norway · Denmark (Greenland) · Finland · Sweden · Iceland

6 Permanent Participants — Indigenous organizations with full participation rights in all working groups and negotiations:

  • ICC — Inuit Circumpolar Council ~180,000 Inuit across Canada, Alaska, Greenland & Russia
  • AIA — Aleut International Association Unangan/Aleut peoples, Alaska & Russia
  • AAC — Arctic Athabaskan Council Athabaskan peoples, Canada & Alaska
  • GCI — Gwich'in Council International Gwich'in peoples, Canada & Alaska
  • RAIPON — Russian Association of Indigenous Peoples of the North
  • Saami Council — Saami peoples, Norway · Sweden · Finland · Russia

② Five Eyes

Multilateral intelligence-sharing alliance. Canada, United States, United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand. Established post-WWII, formalized through bilateral UKUSA Agreement extensions.

③ USMCA

United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement. Free trade agreement in force since July 2020, successor to NAFTA. Governs ~$1.7 trillion in annual trilateral trade.

NORAD

North American Aerospace Defense Command. Canada–US binational command for aerospace warning, aerospace control, and maritime warning. Established 1958.

La Francophonie (OIF)

International Organisation of La Francophonie. 88 member states and governments. Canada is a founding member; Quebec and New Brunswick participate separately.
